Morphir.Myriad bundled third-party components
===========================================

Myriad 0.85.0 is bundled with its upstream runtime assets. Its bundled
FSharp.Core.dll is replaced with FSharp.Core 11.0.100 for ABI compatibility
with the .NET 10 backend. The upstream host source is otherwise unchanged.
